## ABOUT LPU
Lovely Professional University (LPU) is one of the largest private universities in India, located in Phagwara, Punjab. Established in 2005 and recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC), LPU has a massive 600-acre campus. It offers 200+ programs across Engineering, Management, Sciences, Law, Design, Agriculture, Pharmacy, and more. LPU has 40,000+ students from 50+ countries and 2000+ faculty members. It is known for excellent placement record, modern infrastructure, and strong industry connections.

## ADMISSION PROCESS
Step 1 - Registration: Visit www.lpu.in or admission.lpu.in and fill the online application form. Pay the registration fee online.

Step 2 - Entrance Test (LPUNEST): Appear for LPUNEST (LPU National Entrance and Scholarship Test). This test is both an entrance exam and a scholarship gateway. LPUNEST is held in multiple phases throughout the year from December to September. Results are declared within 3 working days of appearing. Students can also use national exam scores like JEE Main, CUET, CAT, CLAT, NEET depending on the program.

Step 3 - Document Submission: Submit required documents including 10th and 12th marksheets, transfer certificate, character certificate, passport-size photographs, and Aadhar card. SC/ST/OBC students must submit caste certificate.

Step 4 - Counseling and Seat Allotment: After qualifying the entrance test, candidates participate in counseling for seat allocation.

Step 5 - Fee Payment: Pay the semester fee to confirm the seat.

Important Admission Dates 2026:
- LPUNEST Phase 5 registration started: August 1, 2026
- LPUNEST Phase 5 exams from: August 4, 2026
- Last date for admission: August 20, 2026
- Scholarship exam last date: August 22, 2026

Eligibility:
- B.Tech: 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics with minimum 60% marks
- B.Com/BBA/B.Sc: 10+2 in any stream with minimum 55% marks
- MBA: Graduation in any stream with minimum 55% marks

Early application allows students to avail scholarships and early bird discounts.

Special Category Scholarships:
- Sports achievements: National/international level athletes
- Cultural achievements: Outstanding performers
- R&D contributions
- Social service awards
- Bravery awards
- Scholarships for orphans and persons with disabilities
- Scholarships for students from Defence and Armed Forces families

Need-Based Financial Aid:
- Available for students from families with specific annual income limits
- Apply with income certificate during admission

How to apply for scholarships:
1. Complete registration on admission.lpu.in
2. Fill the online scholarship form
3. Upload supporting documents (marksheets, income certificate, sports/cultural certificates)
4. University verifies documents and confirms eligibility

## EXAM SCHEDULE AND ACADEMIC CALENDAR
LPU follows a semester system with two semesters per year.

Odd Semester (July to November):
- Classes begin: Last week of July
- Mid-term exams: September
- End-term exams: November to December
- Results: Within 30-45 days

Even Semester (January to May):
- Classes begin: Second week of January
- Mid-term exams: March
- End-term exams: May
- Results: Within 30-45 days

Important Rules:
- Minimum 75% attendance is mandatory to appear in final exams
- Hall tickets are issued online through UMS (University Management System) portal one week before exams
- Supplementary exams held in June for students who fail (re-appear fee applicable)
- Internal assessment includes assignments, quizzes, and class tests
- Theory and practical exams are conducted separately

Students must carry their hall ticket and college ID card to every exam.
