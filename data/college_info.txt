## 🏫 UNIVERSITY OVERVIEW & HISTORY
- About LPU: Lovely Professional University (LPU) is a premier private university established in 2005 under the Lovely Professional University Act by the State Legislature of Punjab. The origin of the institution began in 2001 with the first educational institution, leading to formal UGC Recognition in 2006 under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act, 1956.
- Official Tagline: "Everything an LPU student needs, in one place."
- Campus Size: Sprawling 600+ acre green, Wi-Fi enabled, hi-tech campus situated on the National Highway 1 (GT Road) in Phagwara, Punjab (between Jalandhar and Ludhiana).
- Student Demographics: Over 35,000+ to 40,000+ full-time students from all 28 states of India and 8 union territories, plus 3,000+ international students from over 50+ nations across the globe.
- Incubated Startups: 250+ student and faculty startups supported across 3 on-campus startup incubators.
- Industry Collaborations: 200+ global corporate and academic tie-ups.
- Vision: To be a premier academic institution recognized globally for its contribution to industry and society through excellence in teaching, learning, research, internationalization, entrepreneurship, and leadership.
- Mission: 
  * Transform education through academic rigor, practical learning, and industry integration.
  * Foster research, intellectual innovation, and patent commercialization.
  * Provide an inclusive and diverse multi-cultural learning ecosystem.
  * Develop socially responsible global citizens and entrepreneurial leaders.
- Key Leadership: Chancellor Dr. Ashok Kumar Mittal and Pro-Chancellor Mrs. Rashmi Mittal.

## 📚 COURSES & CURRICULUM STRUCTURE
- Choice Based Credit System (CBCS): LPU implements UGC-recommended CBCS allowing students to customize their learning pathways with core courses, department electives, open electives, and skill enhancement courses.
- Minor Specializations: Engineering and Management students can earn a Minor in another discipline (e.g., a Mechanical student taking a Minor in Computer Science / AI, or a B.Tech student taking a Minor in Finance).
- Industry Certifications & MOOCs: Integration of Coursera, edX, AWS, Google Cloud, Cisco, and Microsoft certifications directly into the curriculum for course credits.
- Grading System: 10-point scale grading system:
  * Grade O (Outstanding): Grade Point 10
  * Grade A+ (Excellent): Grade Point 9
  * Grade A (Very Good): Grade Point 8
  * Grade B+ (Good): Grade Point 7
  * Grade B (Above Average): Grade Point 6
  * Grade C (Average): Grade Point 5
  * Grade P (Pass): Grade Point 4
  * Grade F / E (Fail / Reappear): Grade Point 0
  * Grade I (Incomplete / Ineligible due to attendance): Grade Point 0
- CGPA Calculation: Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is computed based on weighted sum of credit points earned across all semesters divided by total credits registered.
- Capstone Project / Industry Internship: Final year students must undertake a live industry capstone project or a full-semester / year-long industrial internship.

## 📅 ACADEMIC CALENDAR & SCHEDULES
LPU follows a bi-semester system:

1. Autumn / Odd Semester (July/August to December):
   - Course Registration & Fee Payment: June - July
   - Commencement of Classes: Late July / First week of August
   - Mid-Term Tests (MTT): Late September to early October
   - End-Term Practical Exams: Mid-November
   - End-Term Theory Examinations (ETE): Late November to Mid-December
   - Winter Break: Mid-December to first week of January

2. Spring / Even Semester (January to May/June):
   - Course Registration: Late December - Early January
   - Commencement of Classes: Second week of January
   - Mid-Term Tests (MTT): Late February to early March
   - End-Term Practical Exams: Mid-April
   - End-Term Theory Examinations (ETE): May to early June
   - Summer Break / Summer Term: June to July (Summer term allows students to clear backlogs or accelerate credits).

3. Holidays & Observances:
   - All gazetted national and state holidays (Independence Day, Republic Day, Gandhi Jayanti, Diwali, Holi, Gurpurab, Eid, Christmas).
   - Academic calendars with exact dates are published on UMS (ums.lpu.in) under the "Academic Calendar" tab prior to each session.

## 📝 EXAMINATIONS, ASSESSMENTS & RESULTS
- Evaluation Pattern:
  * Continuous Assessment (CA) [20% - 30% weightage]: Includes objective quizzes on UMS, home assignments, class participation, case studies, coding assignments, and term papers.
  * Mid-Term Test (MTT) [20% - 25% weightage]: Conducted midway through the semester covering 50% syllabus.
  * End-Term Examination (ETE) [50% - 60% weightage]: Comprehensive examination at the end of the semester covering the entire syllabus.
  * Practical & Lab Evaluation: Continuous lab performance + viva-voce + end-term lab experiment.
- Examination Eligibility:
  * Minimum 75% aggregate attendance is mandatory in each individual course to be eligible to sit for End-Term Exams.
  * Clear all pending fee dues and possess an approved online Admit Card generated via UMS.
- Admit Card & Seating Plan:
  * Admit cards are downloadable from UMS 5–7 days before exam commencement.
  * Detailed seating plan with Block, Room Number, and Desk Number is updated daily on the UMS App and notice boards.
- Reappear / Backlog Policy:
  * Students receiving 'F' or 'E' grade must register for Reappear Examination through UMS by paying the prescribed reappear fee (approx. Rs. 300 - Rs. 500 per subject).
  * Reappear exams are held along with regular ETE in the subsequent semester or during the Summer Term.
- Answer Script Scrutiny & Revaluation:
  * Scrutiny: Students can inspect evaluated answer scripts on scheduled post-exam inspection days.
  * Revaluation: If unsatisfied, students can apply for formal revaluation via UMS within the specified deadline (revaluation fee applies).
- Unfair Means (UFM): Strict zero-tolerance policy against cheating, carrying unauthorized papers, mobile phones, or smartwatches into examination halls. UFM committee penalties range from cancellation of paper to semester rustication.

## 🤖 AI FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Q1: What is the minimum attendance required at LPU to write exams?
A1: Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered subject. If attendance drops below 75%, the student becomes debarred (Grade I) and cannot sit for the End-Term Exam for that subject.

Q2: How can I apply for LPUNEST scholarship?
A2: Register on admission.lpu.in, book your preferred exam slot, and appear for the LPUNEST test. Based on your test score bracket (Category I, II, or III), you will be awarded tuition fee waivers up to 50-60%.

Q3: What are the library timings at LPU?
A3: The Dr. S. Radhakrishnan Central Library (Block 15) is open daily from 8:00 AM to 12:00 Midnight. During exam periods, reading halls remain open 24 hours.

Q4: What is the procedure for taking hostel leave or a gate pass?
A4: Log into the UMS App / Portal, navigate to "Hostel Leave / Gate Pass", fill in leave dates and reason, and submit. The system sends an automated approval request to the parent's registered contact. Once approved, an electronic gate pass QR code is generated.

Q5: What are the highest and average placement packages at LPU?
A5: The highest international package is Rs. 3 Crore per annum, and the highest domestic package is Rs. 64 LPA. The average package for Engineering/Computer Science graduates ranges from Rs. 6.5 to 8.5 LPA.

Q6: How is CGPA calculated at LPU?
A6: LPU uses a 10-point scale (Grade O = 10, A+ = 9, A = 8, B+ = 7, B = 6, C = 5, P = 4, F/E/I = 0). SGPA and CGPA are computed as the sum of (Course Credit × Grade Point Earned) divided by the total registered credits.

Q7: What is the difference between MTT and ETE?
A7: MTT (Mid-Term Test) is conducted midway through the semester covering 50% syllabus (approx. 20-25% weightage). ETE (End-Term Examination) is conducted at semester end covering the entire syllabus (approx. 50-60% weightage).

Q8: Can I change my room or hostel after allotment?
A8: Room change requests can be submitted through UMS during designated room change windows at the start of the academic semester, subject to room availability and Chief Warden approval.

Q9: What happens if I fail a subject at LPU?
A9: If you receive an 'F' or 'E' grade, you must apply for a Reappear Examination via UMS by paying the nominal reappear fee. You can clear the reappear exam in the next semester's ETE or during the summer term.

Q10: Where is the UniHospital located and what services does it offer?
A10: UniHospital is located on campus and offers 24x7 emergency medical care, resident doctors, OPD, diagnostic lab, ICU, ambulances, and an attached pharmacy. Basic consultation is free for enrolled students.

## 🎒 FRESHER'S STARTING GUIDE & FIRST-MONTH ESSENTIALS
Everything a new student and parent needs to know in the first 30 days:

1. Day 1 Move-In & Shopping for Essentials:
   - Where to buy mattress, buckets, pillows, locks, and bedsheets: Available immediately inside UniMall (Ground Floor) and in departmental stores located inside every hostel courtyard. Complete fresher bedding kits are sold on campus so students do not need to carry heavy luggage.
   - Room Key & Allotment: Check your room allotment on UMS / Admission portal, visit your designated hostel block helpdesk, meet the warden, and collect your room keys.

2. Campus 24x7 Wi-Fi Setup:
   - Connect your phone/laptop to the Wi-Fi network named "LPU-WiFi".
   - Open any browser; the captive login portal will automatically pop up.
   - Login using your Student Registration Number (e.g., 121XXXXX) and the Wi-Fi password provided during admission/UMS setup.

3. Internal Campus Transit:
   - Walking avenues connect all academic blocks, hostels, and UniMall.
   - Campus E-Rickshaws operate continuously between main gates, academic blocks, and hostels for a nominal fare of ₹10 to ₹15.
   - Personal bicycles are allowed and can be parked at designated cycle stands outside each block.

4. Gate Pass & Weekend Outing Procedure:
   - Hostels close between 7:00 PM and 8:00 PM daily.
   - To visit Phagwara or Jalandhar for shopping on weekends: Open the UMS / LPU Touch App ➔ Go to "Hostel Leave / Gate Pass" ➔ Select "Day Pass" or "Home Leave" ➔ Enter date & reason ➔ Submit.
   - An automated IVR approval call/SMS is triggered to the parent's registered mobile number. Once parent approves, an electronic QR code is generated on the student's phone.
   - Scan the QR code at the turnstile exit gates at Main Gate.

5. Class Timetables & Classrooms:
   - Log into UMS (ums.lpu.in) or LPU Touch App ➔ Click "Class Timetable".
   - Check your Section Code, Teacher Name, and Room Number (e.g., "34-402" means Block 34, 4th Floor, Room 402).

6. Best Pocket-Friendly Food Spots:
   - UniMall 4th & 5th Floor: Multi-cuisine food court featuring Domino's, Subway, Chaayos, Roll Xpress, South Indian mess, and Chinese stalls.
   - Block 34 & 38 Food Streets: Highly popular student food stalls serving parathas, momos, fresh juices, and coffee.
   - Hostel Night Canteens: Open until late night for midnight study snacks.

7. Joining Student Clubs & Societies:
   - Over 100+ student organizations and technical societies operate under Division of Student Welfare (DSW - Block 30).
   - Freshers can register during the annual "Club Induction Fair" held during the first month at Shanti Devi Mittal Auditorium (Google Developer Groups, IEEE, Robotics Club, Dance, Theatre, Photography, Music).
